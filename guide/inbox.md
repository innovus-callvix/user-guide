# Inbox

The Inbox is your unified communication hub — every call and SMS conversation with your contacts lives here in one place. No switching between separate call logs and message threads.

![Inbox overview](screenshots/inbox-overview.png)
*Left panel: conversation list — Right panel: the active conversation*

---

## Layout

The Inbox has three panels:

| Panel | What it shows |
|-------|--------------|
| **Left** | List of all conversations, sorted by most recent activity |
| **Centre** | The full conversation — call history, SMS messages, notes |
| **Right** | Contact profile, call details, and quick actions |

---

## Starting a new conversation

Click **New Message** (top-right of the Inbox) to open the new message dialog.

![New message dialog](screenshots/inbox-new-message.png)
*Search for a contact or type a new number, choose your From number, and type your message*

1. Search for an existing contact or type a phone number.
2. Choose which of your numbers to send from.
3. Type your message and hit **Send**.

To make a call instead, use the floating **dialer** in the bottom-left corner.

---

## Conversations

Each conversation groups **all communication** — calls and SMS — with a specific contact into a single thread. You can see the full history at a glance.

![Conversation view](screenshots/inbox-conversation.png)
*Calls show as call cards with duration and outcome; messages show as chat bubbles*

**Within a conversation you can:**
- Reply with an SMS or MMS (attach an image with the paperclip icon)
- See all past calls and their recording status
- Add a private **note** visible only to your team
- View and edit the contact's profile in the right panel

---

## Filtering conversations

Use the **filter** icon at the top of the conversation list to narrow down by:

- **Status** — All, Unread, Read
- **Type** — Calls, Messages, or both
- **Assigned agent**
- **Phone number**

![Conversation filters](screenshots/inbox-filters.png)

---

## Call cards

When a call appears in a conversation, its card shows:

- Direction (inbound / outbound)
- Duration
- Outcome (answered, missed, voicemail)
- Recording (click to play inline)
- Transcript (click to expand)

![Call card](screenshots/inbox-call-card.png)
*Click the play button to listen to a recording directly in the conversation*

---

## Notes

Notes let your team leave internal comments on a conversation — the contact never sees them.

1. Click the **Notes** tab inside a conversation.
2. Type your note and press **Save**.
3. Notes are visible to all team members with access to that conversation.

![Conversation notes](screenshots/inbox-notes.png)

---

## Smart reply

While composing a reply, click **Suggest** to get AI-generated response options based on the conversation context. Click any suggestion to insert it into the message box — edit it before sending.

---

## Marking as read / unread

Right-click any conversation (or use the **⋯** menu) and select **Mark as read** or **Mark as unread**. Unread conversations show a blue dot and are counted in the Inbox badge.
