# Insights & Reports

Insights give admins and owners a view into how your team is performing — call volumes, response times, SMS activity, and voicemail trends.

Go to **More → Insights** to access all reports.

![Insights overview](screenshots/insights-overview.png)

---

## Call Report

The Call Report shows all calls across your workspace for a selected time period.

![Call report](screenshots/insights-call-report.png)

**Filter by:**
- Date range
- Phone number
- Agent
- Direction (inbound / outbound)
- Status (completed, missed, failed)

**What you'll see:**
- Total calls, answered calls, missed calls, and average duration
- A breakdown by day or hour (bar chart)
- A table of every call with caller, agent, duration, and outcome

Click **Export** to download the full report as a CSV.

---

## SMS Report

The SMS Report tracks all messages sent and received.

![SMS report](screenshots/insights-sms-report.png)

**Filter by:**
- Date range
- Phone number
- Direction (inbound / outbound)

**What you'll see:**
- Total messages sent and received
- Daily message volume chart
- Per-number breakdown

Click **Export** to download as CSV.

---

## Voicemail Report

The Voicemail Report shows all voicemails received across your numbers.

![Voicemail report](screenshots/insights-voicemail-report.png)

**What you'll see:**
- Total voicemails received
- Which numbers are getting the most voicemails
- Which agents are assigned to those numbers
- Trend over time

Voicemails with recordings can be played directly from the report table.

Click **Export** to download as CSV.

---

## Live Call Monitor

For a real-time view of active calls right now, go to **Live Calls** in the sidebar.

![Live call monitor](screenshots/live-calls.png)
*Each card shows the caller, agent, phone number, and how long the call has been active — all updating live*

The live monitor is useful for:
- Supervisors watching team activity
- Spotting long or stuck calls
- Seeing which agents are currently busy
